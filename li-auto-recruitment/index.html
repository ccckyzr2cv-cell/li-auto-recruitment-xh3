Senior Emotor Mechanical Expert

Engineering

Munich, Germany · Full-time

View full job description

About the team

Our “Electric Powertrain” team focuses on the advanced technologies in electric powertrain field, including powertrain and powernet system architectures, different integration solutions, power electronic technologies (inverter, charger, DC/DC etc.), new types of e-machine, innovated gearbox solutions and new component technologies.

We are a mixed team with experts from different technical fields, committed to innovation together and to delivering advanced electric powertrain related technologies for long-term product competitiveness.

We welcome candidates who are passionate about pushing the boundaries of powertrain transmission and eager to contribute to our pioneering work. Join us on this exciting journey and make a significant impact in the field of powertrain transmission.

Duties and responsibilities

  1. Core Technology Leadership and Research
    1. Lead the mechanical design and integrated development of motors (stators, rotors, end covers, bearings and other core components).
    2. Formulate motor mechanical design specifications, technical standards and development processes.
    3. Take the lead in solving complex mechanical engineering problems (such as vibration and noise, structural strength, assembly accuracy, sealing reliability, etc.) in the process of motor R&D, production and application.
  2. Technological Innovation and Cutting-Edge Technology Implementation
    1. Track global technical development trends, industry standards and competitor dynamics in the motor mechanical field (motor structure, lightweight materials, precision manufacturing, assembly technology, etc.).
    2. Take the lead in carrying out pre-research on cutting-edge technologies (such as new motor structures, lightweight design and high-efficiency assembly schemes).
    3. Promote the transformation of technological innovation achievements into product applications, form core technology patents and technical barriers, and enhance the company’s technical competitiveness.
  3. Technical System and Capability Development
    1. Take the lead in building the company’s motor mechanical technology system.
    2. Formulate technical development plans, improve technical documents such as motor mechanical design manuals, fault case databases and test standards, and promote technology standardization.
    3. Be responsible for team technical training, guidance and talent training; improve the team’s overall motor mechanical design, technical research and engineering practice capabilities; and build a professional and efficient technical team.

Requirements

  1. Education/Experience
    1. Master’s degree or above, major in Mechanical Design and Manufacturing and Automation, Mechanical Engineering, Vehicle Engineering, Power Machinery and Engineering or related majors; PhD or 5+ years of experience at a leading OEM or Tier-1/2 supplier preferred.
    2. Experience in industry-leading product development and mass production is preferred.
    3. Successful electric motor development experience is required; products developed to mass-production status are preferred.
  2. Knowledge
    1. Proficient in mechanical design principles, structural optimization methods and manufacturing processes of motors (stators, rotors, end covers, bearings, etc.), core technologies such as motor structural strength, vibration and noise control, sealing design and assembly-accuracy control, and the selection and application of lightweight materials.
    2. Familiar with manufacturing processes of core motor mechanical components, including machining, die casting, injection molding and welding; with cost awareness and design-for-manufacturability capabilities.
    3. Familiar with relevant motor-mechanics industry standards, motor product testing and verification; able to formulate test plans, analyze test data and promote closed-loop resolution of test problems. Familiarity with DFMEA and PFMEA is preferred.
  3. Skills and Competencies
    1. Passionate about engineering, with a strong spirit of innovation.
    2. Good team worker with a strong sense of responsibility and strong cross-cultural communication skills.
    3. Proficient in at least one mainstream 3D CAD design software (such as CATIA, SOLIDWORKS or UG) and CAE simulation tools (such as ANSYS or ABAQUS).
    4. Proactive, with a pioneering spirit and a good sense of internal and external client.
    5. Good command of written and spoken English.

About Li Auto

We’re a leading Chinese new energy vehicle company that designs, develops, manufactures and sells premium smart electric vehicles (EVs). Through product, technology and business model innovations, we provide families with safer, more comfortable and more convenient products and services.

Engineering

Munich, Germany · Full-time

View full job description

About the team

Our Semiconductor Advanced Research team develops next-generation semiconductor, packaging and application technologies for electric vehicle and embodied AI products. We combine system-level design, advanced packaging, simulation, testing and failure analysis to transform early-stage concepts into validated demonstrators.

We are looking for a hands-on, self-driven engineer with strong expertise in power or MEMS semiconductor modules, packaging technologies, and electro-thermal-mechanical co-design. You will work in a cross-functional and international team to develop, validate and continuously improve advanced semiconductor-module solutions.

Duties and responsibilities

  1. Design and develop power semiconductor packages and modules, from concept definition through prototype build and validation.
  2. Lead or contribute to electro-thermal-mechanical and multiphysics co-design of semiconductor-module solutions.
  3. Work closely with electrical, thermal, mechanical, simulation and process-development experts to develop robust, manufacturable design concepts.
  4. Define and maintain module-level bills of materials, including substrates, copper materials, bonding wires, encapsulation materials, thermal-interface materials and related packaging components.
  5. Create and maintain 3D component and assembly models, drawings and technical documentation using CAD tools.
  6. Plan, execute and evaluate electrical, thermal, mechanical and reliability testing of semiconductor modules.
  7. Support development and assembly of functional prototypes and demonstrators.
  8. Initiate and coordinate failure-analysis activities with internal teams and external partners, including universities and laboratories; translate findings into design improvements.
  9. Evaluate emerging semiconductors, packaging and application technologies relevant to EV and embodied AI products.
  10. Contribute technical input to design reviews, supplier discussions, technology roadmaps and cross-functional decision-making.

Requirements

  1. Master’s degree or PhD in microelectronics, power electronics, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, thermal engineering, materials science or a related discipline.
  2. Several years of hands-on experience in semiconductor-package, power-module, electronics or electromechanical-component design and development.
  3. Experience with PCB design tools such as Altium Designer or Cadence and 3D CAD tools such as CATIA or SolidWorks, including development of circuit schematic component and assembly models.
  4. Practical experience with semiconductor-module testing and characterization, such as double-pulse testing, short-circuit testing, static characterization, thermal testing or reliability testing.
  5. Basic understanding of semiconductor-module structures, materials, interconnects, thermal paths and packaging processes.
  6. Takes ownership of problems and outcomes, addressing issues transparently and constructively through root-cause analysis and corrective action rather than deflecting responsibility.
  7. Comfortable working in an ambiguous and fast-moving R&D environment; able to prioritize effectively, make sound decisions and maintain momentum across multiple stakeholders.
  8. Highly self-motivated and proactive; able to identify opportunities, risks and gaps independently and drive work forward without close supervision.
  9. Constructive, collaborative communicator who raises problems early, engages the right stakeholders and focuses discussions on solutions and next steps.
  10. Professional fluency in written and spoken English; Chinese is a plus.

Junior Digital Modeler – Advanced Design

Design

Munich, Germany · Full-time

View full job description

About Us

We are an industry-leading mobility innovation team dedicated to exploring future lifestyles and mobility solutions for the next 5 to 15 years. Based in Munich—a global hub blending Bavarian heritage with world-class automotive excellence—our Advanced Design Center aims to unite top creative talents to break conventional boundaries and define the next generation of design language for transportation.

Role Overview

We are seeking a passionate and detail-oriented Junior Digital Modeler to join our Advanced Design team in Munich. In this role, you will act as the critical bridge between creative sketches and engineering reality. You will be responsible for building high-quality preliminary Class-A surfaces (CAS).

Beyond technical proficiency, you must possess a keen design sensitivity to accurately capture designers’ intent, coordinate external digital vendors, and ensure the highest fidelity in translating visionary concepts into digital reality.

Key Responsibilities

  1. CAS Modeling & Surfacing
    1. Translate hand sketches, renderings and clay-scan data into high-quality 3D digital models (CAS) using Blender and Alias.
    2. Participate in the full iteration cycle from early concept to full-scale clay-model support, ensuring rapid response to design changes and precise control over proportions and stance.
  2. Vendor & Project Coordination
    1. Serve as the primary liaison for external digital-modeling vendors, potentially across different time zones. Manage briefing processes, review deliverables for quality assurance and ensure timely milestone deliveries.
    2. Assist in digital asset management and archiving; track project progress and escalate potential risks proactively.
  3. Design Support
    1. Prepare models for visualization and rendering; utilize 3D data to evaluate proportions, posture and feature lines.
    2. Participate in design reviews, providing constructive feedback on model feasibility to aid design decision-making.

Qualifications

  1. Education: Bachelor’s degree or higher in Transportation Design, Automotive Design, Industrial Design or a related field.
  2. Technical Skills: Expert-level Blender skills are mandatory, with the ability to construct complex surfacing and forms.
  3. Knowledge of Alias: Foundational understanding and operational experience with Autodesk Alias; awareness of its application in the automotive pipeline.
  4. Design Sensitivity: Strong aesthetic judgment with a sharp eye for proportion, volume, lighting and detailing.
  5. Industry Knowledge: Familiarity with the end-to-end automotive design development process, particularly within the Advanced Design phase.
  6. Soft Skills: Excellent logical thinking and spatial visualization abilities.
  7. Language: Fluency in English is required; fluency in Chinese is a significant plus.
  8. Strong team player with effective cross-functional and cross-cultural communication skills.
  9. Experience: Open to recent graduates or candidates with 1–2 years of relevant experience. A portfolio demonstrating modeling process and final outputs is mandatory.
